SmartStep AI Leather Shoe

“Smart Steps. Better Health.”

A smart leather shoe concept that blends durable, comfortable footwear with embedded sensors and AI-based pattern analysis. It is designed to monitor foot pressure, temperature and movement, and to give a potential early warning when readings look unusual.

The Problem

Foot changes can go unnoticed until they become serious

For many people, small changes in the feet are easy to miss. For those at risk of diabetic foot complications, regular monitoring matters, but it is not always easy to do.

Early changes are easy to miss

People may not notice early changes in foot condition, such as slight temperature or pressure differences, during busy daily life.

Some people need regular checks

People at risk of diabetic foot complications may require regular foot monitoring as part of preventive care.

Monitoring can be inconvenient and costly

Frequent visits and specialised equipment can be inconvenient and expensive for many households.

Daily activity goes unmonitored

Many people do not continuously monitor their feet while walking, standing and working through the day.

Our Solution

SmartStep AI Leather Shoe is designed to monitor the foot during everyday wear and flag unusual patterns early, without changing how a quality leather shoe feels.

How It Works

From a single step to a useful prompt

Five simple stages turn raw sensor readings into a clear message for the wearer.

  1. 1

    Sensors collect data

    Pressure, temperature and motion sensors in the insole gather readings as the wearer moves.

  2. 2

    Data is processed

    A small processing unit filters noise and organises the readings into clean, time-stamped data.

  3. 3

    AI analyses patterns

    The analysis looks at how readings change over time and compares them with the wearer’s usual pattern.

  4. 4

    Abnormal readings trigger an alert

    If an unusual pattern is detected, the system is designed to raise a potential early warning.

  5. 5

    User takes appropriate action

    The wearer can check their foot, adjust activity, or seek professional medical advice.

AI Health Monitoring

How AI can spot unusual patterns

Instead of looking at one reading, AI can look at patterns over time, such as a slow rise in temperature combined with repeated high pressure in one area.

Sensor DataPressure, temperature, movement
AI AnalysisLearns the wearer’s usual pattern
Pattern DetectionFinds readings that stand out
AlertPrompts the wearer to check

In simple terms

  • The system builds an idea of what is “usual” for the wearer.
  • It compares new readings against that usual pattern.
  • Repeated or growing differences can raise a potential early warning.
  • The wearer is encouraged to check their foot and seek professional advice if concerned.

Important: the AI in this concept is a monitoring aid. It is not a medical diagnosis system and cannot replace a doctor or podiatrist.

Target Market

Who SmartStep is designed for

First priority

People at risk of diabetic foot complications

People who may benefit from regular foot checks as part of preventive care.

First priority

People requiring regular foot monitoring

Anyone advised by a health professional to keep a closer eye on foot condition.

Healthcare organizations

Organizations that support patient prevention and follow-up programmes.

Clinics and hospitals

Facilities that could supply the shoe as an additional monitoring tool for patients.

Elderly users

Older adults who may find it harder to check their own feet regularly.

Occupational workers

People who spend long periods standing or walking and place sustained load on their feet.

Why start with people who need regular foot-health monitoring?

  • Clear need: monitoring is already part of their care routine, so the value of the shoe is easy to understand.
  • Focused design: one core user group lets us tune sensors and alerts for a specific purpose.
  • Strong partnerships: clinics and health organizations are natural early partners for this group.
  • Better evidence: focused pilots can generate the data needed to improve and validate the product responsibly.

Impact

Potential impact

These are the outcomes we aim to work towards. They are goals, not proven results.

Health impact

  • Potential earlier awareness of unusual foot patterns.
  • Support for preventive habits and timely professional consultation.
  • A tool that complements, and does not replace, regular medical care.

Social impact

  • Make health monitoring more accessible through everyday footwear.
  • Help older adults and busy workers keep an eye on foot health.
  • Raise awareness about foot care and prevention.

Economic impact

  • Potential to reduce the cost of late-stage treatment through earlier action.
  • New value-added product line for footwear makers.
  • Potential skilled jobs in design, assembly, electronics and support.

Technology impact

  • Practical application of sensors and AI in a familiar everyday product.
  • A platform for local innovation in wearable health technology.
  • Opportunity to build data-driven, privacy-respecting health tools.

Leather-industry impact

  • Higher-value use of locally made leather and footwear.
  • New demand for skilled leather and shoemaking work.
  • A way to connect traditional craft with modern technology.
Our Vision

To make health monitoring more accessible by turning everyday footwear into a quiet partner in prevention, so that every step can help people stay healthier.
